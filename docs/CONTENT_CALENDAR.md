# Content Calendar

## Publishing Rhythm

Target: 4 high-quality SEO guides per month.

Priority order:

1. Price and cost questions.
2. Operational pain questions.
3. Local service intent pages.
4. Comparison and decision guides.

## Monthly Workflow

Week 1:
- Review Search Console queries.
- Pick 4 article topics.
- Confirm no duplicate intent.

Week 2:
- Draft 2 articles.
- Add internal links to service pages.
- Validate metadata and schema.

Week 3:
- Draft 2 articles.
- Update homepage guide teaser if a new article is stronger.

Week 4:
- Review rankings, impressions, clicks, WhatsApp leads.
- Update older content based on real client questions.
